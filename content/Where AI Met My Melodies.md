I have always wanted to write songs and set them to music. Over the years, I recorded plenty of voice notes—melodies, fragments of lyrics—but they never quite sounded like what I imagined a finished album would be.

Then came the inexorable rise of AI across industries. Somewhere along the way, I discovered an app that lets me sing my ideas, and it transforms them into fully produced songs. Suddenly, those rough recordings had the potential to become something real.

Here are my first two songs. An album? That’s definitely somewhere in the plan :) 

Stay tuned. 🎶
